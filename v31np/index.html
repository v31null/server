The drop-in of PS-N/PF-1032, the V31NP NETWORK STANDARD, is the ready-to-use function of the Bureau for Onion & Over of þͤ Services of þe General populace Ministeria custodiae filaciorum ( Secura ), by which a site serves its pages and files by the exchange of .v31np files.

The drop-in is one folder, v31np, which a site places in the root folder of its host. It serves a PHP server and a Node.js server alike. One command, of PHP or of Node.js, builds the archive of the site.

A page carries in its head a script element whose source is /v31np/v31np.js and whose attributes data-archive and data-url name the archive and its URL, or answers per query by a single call at its top.
